03 / How I work

Hands-on delivery.
Decisions you can follow.

I start with existing behavior, integration boundaries and the constraints the team has to live with. Then I look for the smallest useful change that makes the next one easier.

  • Make ownership explicit. Keep domain state, rendering and external integrations understandable.
  • Check the real workflow. Use regression tests, browser checks and package-consumer validation where they matter.
  • Leave a usable trail. Document the decision, its tradeoffs and how to verify it.

I use AI tools for implementation and investigation, with source review and verification. My public samples identify their scope and the evidence behind them.
